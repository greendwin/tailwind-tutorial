Why we rewrote our design system in a weekend, and what we learned

Grace Hopper · March 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Every growing product eventually hits the same wall: dozens of slightly different buttons, five shades of "brand blue", and a stylesheet nobody dares to delete from. We hit that wall last spring.

Instead of another round of cleanup tickets, we tried something radical: a strict set of design tokens and a utility-first workflow. Here's how it went.

What changed

The biggest wins came from constraints, not from new features:

  • One spacing scale used everywhere
  • Three font weights instead of seven
  • Colors referenced by role, never by hex code
"The best design decision is the one you only have to make once."
